  • A housing development — also donated

Population & demographics

Who lives here

  • Half are college students — flats and a student clinic hug the campus
  • About a third are families — houses cluster around the K-12
  • One in five is 65+ — senior living and hospice sit in the quiet east
  • That mix is why the map looks the way it does

The anchor · donated, off-budget

University School of Medicine

  • Med school, the Marchand library, and Whitmore + Caldecott halls around one quad
  • Sits at the town’s center — every district zones outward from it
  • Student flats wrap the west and south edges
  • It’s why the town exists, so it gets the best spot
#3–15 · Wellness Way

Thirteen ways to get care

  • Emergency: Havenbrook General + Hartline EMS, a block apart on the Wellness spine
  • Everyday: Thacher clinic, Brookfield physicians, Marigold dental, Parallax optical, Argus labs
  • Long-term: Stoneleigh rehab, Stillwater behavioral health, Halcyon House, Stillpoint hospice
  • Public health: the county Health Department · At home: Innisfree visits
#2 · Residential West · donated, off-budget

The Preserve

  • The required housing development — cottages, duplexes, townhouses
  • Preserve Commons apartments fill the middle of the neighborhood
  • Scholar’s Walk + University Lofts take students; Midtown Flats takes downtown
  • Nobody is more than a short walk from campus or a bus route
Community · 11 locations

The parts that aren’t medicine

  • Errands: Havenbrook Commons mall, Masterson’s, Olsen’s Market, Bellwether Pharmacy
  • Food: The Orchard Table, The Painted Coyote, The Whippoorwill coffeehouse
  • Outside: Willow Creek Park — pond, trails, bandshell
  • The boring-but-needed: post office, school district, Quarry Hill Museum
#14–15 · the quiet east

A place to grow old

  • Halcyon House — assisted living + memory care around a courtyard garden
  • Stillpoint Hospice next door, so the hardest visits stay short and private
  • Innisfree home health means seniors keep their own homes longer
  • The whole block sits on Sunset Ridge — farthest from traffic, closest to quiet
Layout & placement

How we arranged it

  • Streets first: two arterials cross at the campus, 18 named streets fill the grid
  • Hospital and EMS share the Wellness Way spine — minutes from anywhere
  • Shops run along Commerce Blvd; the K-12 sits inside the neighborhoods
  • Every address is within two blocks of a through-street — that’s the EMS rule
Decision-making

What we traded

  • The two donated sites freed ~$4.5M — so we bought 13 small facilities instead of one mega-campus
  • Picked the mall over a second park — the Preserve already covers green space
  • Senior care went to the quiet east instead of paying downtown frontage
  • Stopped at 26 buildings and banked the leftover $50,000 instead of forcing a 27th

Conclusion

Why it works

  • Students get campus, housing, the clinic, and coffee — no car needed
  • Families get the school, pediatricians, groceries, and the park
  • Seniors get the full care loop — home visits to hospice — on one quiet side of town
  • And the whole town lands at $9.95M, under the $10M cap
