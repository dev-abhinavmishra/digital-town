Havenbrook University School of Medicine

  • The Marchand Medical Library, Whitmore Anatomy Hall, and Caldecott Clinical Sciences Hall around one quad
  • Sits at the town’s centre — every district zones outward from it
  • Student flats wrap the west and south edges
  • It’s why the town exists, so it gets the best spot
N ↑University

Thirteen ways to get care

  • Emergency: Havenbrook General Hospital and Garrison Medical Transport, a block apart on the Wellness spine
  • Everyday: Thacher Student Health Clinic, Brookfield Family Physicians, Marigold Dental Studio, Parallax Optical, Meridian Diagnostics Laboratory
  • Long-term: Stoneleigh Rehabilitation Center, Ashwood Behavioral Health Center, Halcyon House Senior Living, Stillpoint Hospice
  • Public health: Havenbrook County Health Department · At home: Innisfree Home Health
  • Why these: every site maps to a resident — students, families, and the 65+ block
N ↑University

The Preserve at Havenbrook

  • The required housing development — cottages, duplexes, townhouses
  • Preserve Commons Apartments fill the middle of the neighborhood
  • Scholar’s Court and Scholar’s Walk apartments plus University Lofts take the students; Midtown Flats takes downtown
  • Nobody is more than a short walk from campus or a bus route

The parts that aren’t medicine

  • Errands: Havenbrook Commons Mall, Bardsley’s Department Store, Olsen’s Market, Bellwether Pharmacy
  • Food: The Orchard Table, Mariposa Cantina, The Whippoorwill Coffeehouse
  • Outside: Willow Creek Park — pond, trails, bandshell
  • The boring-but-needed: Havenbrook Post Office, Havenbrook Unified School District, Quarry Hill Museum

A place to grow old

  • Halcyon House Senior Living — assisted living + memory care around a courtyard garden
  • Stillpoint Hospice next door, so the hardest visits stay short and private
  • Innisfree Home Health means seniors keep their own homes longer
  • The whole block sits on Sunset Ridge — farthest from traffic, closest to quiet
N ↑University

Shopping and entertainment

  • Havenbrook Commons Mall — the anchor of the Commerce Blvd corridor
  • Bardsley’s Department Store and Olsen’s Market for the essentials
  • Quarry Hill Museum on the civic plaza — the rainy-day option
  • Willow Creek Park and The Whippoorwill Coffeehouse for the slower afternoons
N ↑University

How we laid it out

  • Streets first: two arterials cross at the campus, 18 named streets fill the grid
  • Hospital and EMS share the Wellness Way spine — minutes from anywhere
  • Shops run along Commerce Blvd; the K-12 sits inside the neighborhoods
  • Every address is within two blocks of a through-street — that’s the EMS rule
N ↑University

Haven downtown

  • A walkable core where the campus, the hospital district, and the shops share a few busy blocks
  • Students can go class → clinic → coffee without ever needing a car
  • The plaza and the old-town skyline — Pinnacle Health Plaza, Foundry One — give it a real centre
N ↑University

Havenbrook Unified School District

  • The K-12 anchor for the family neighborhoods on the southwest side
  • Health-science electives and athletics feed straight into the university pipeline
  • Sits inside the neighborhoods it serves — kids walk, not bus
N ↑University

What we traded

  • The two donated sites freed ~$4.5M — so we bought 13 small facilities instead of one mega-campus
  • Picked the mall over a second park — the Preserve already covers green space
  • Senior care went to the quiet east instead of paying downtown frontage
  • Stopped at 26 buildings and banked the leftover $50,000 instead of forcing a 27th

Why it works

  • Students get the campus, the flats, the Thacher Student Health Clinic, and the Whippoorwill — no car needed
  • Families get the school, Brookfield Family Physicians, Olsen’s Market, and Willow Creek Park
  • Seniors get the full care loop — Innisfree Home Health visits all the way to Stillpoint Hospice
  • And the whole town lands at $9.95M, under the $10M cap
